# 🔩 **Clamp CAD Directory**
This folder contains all CAD models, assemblies, and reference geometry for the **ModuDrone clamping systems**, including both **motor‑mount clamps** and **arm clamps**.  
These components mechanically secure the propulsion system and structural arms to the fuselage.

---

## 🧱 **What’s Inside**
### **Motor‑Mount Clamps**
- 30 mm carbon‑fiber tube clamp bodies  
- PA612‑CF printable variants  
- M6 heat‑set insert bosses (7–8 mm reinforced)  
- Through‑pin channel geometry for torque‑locking  
- Mounting interfaces for MAD HAVOC motor plates

## 🛠️ **Design Intent**
The clamp subsystem is engineered to:
- Provide rigid, slip‑free attachment of arms and motor tubes  
- Withstand high‑torque loads from large‑format MAD motors  
- Maintain precise thrust‑vector alignment across the V‑Octo layout  
- Support PA612‑CF manufacturing constraints (wall thickness, boss geometry, print orientation)  
- Allow modular replacement and rapid iteration during prototyping

---


## 🔄 **Update Workflow**
1. Modify or create clamp geometry in Onshape  
2. Export STEP files  
3. Commit changes with clear version notes  
4. Add relevant updates to `Updates.md` if clamp geometry affects fuselage, motor spacing, or assembly alignment

---

## 📘 **Notes**
This directory evolves alongside the fuselage and propulsion system.  
Expect frequent updates as clamp strength, weight, and manufacturability are optimized for PA612‑CF printing and high‑thrust MAD motor operation.
