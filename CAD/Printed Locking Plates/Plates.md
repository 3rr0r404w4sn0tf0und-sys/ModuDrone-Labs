# Printed Locking Plates
This folder contains all 3D‑printed locking plates used in the ModuDrone v1 fuselage and module system.

These plates are specifically for **tight or constrained areas** where standard T‑brackets, steel plates, or larger structural connectors cannot fit.  
They provide lightweight reinforcement, alignment, and clamping for small interfaces throughout the drone.

## Included in this folder
- Small locking plates for rib interfaces  
- Clamp plates for sockets and mounts  
- Thin reinforcement plates for narrow structural gaps  
- Cover plates for wiring channels or tight access points  
- Any plate designed to “lock” two parts together where a T‑bracket is too large

## Notes
- All plates in this folder are designed for **3D printing only**  
- Typical print settings: 3 walls, 25–40% gyroid, CF‑Nylon  
- Fasteners vary (M3–M6) depending on the plate’s location  
- These plates are used throughout the fuselage, front module, and rear module

## Purpose of this folder
To keep all **small-format structural locking plates** organized in one place, separate from other parts

This ensures the CAD directory stays clean and modular as the V6 fuselage expands.
